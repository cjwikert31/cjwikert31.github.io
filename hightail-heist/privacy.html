Privacy Policy for Hightail Heist

Effective date: November 22, 2026

Hightail Heist ("the game") is made by ContortedCrag Studios ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the game uses and why.

1. Information we collect ourselves

We do not run our own servers for the game, and we do not collect, store or sell your personal information ourselves. The game uses Google services, described below, which process some information to provide ads, the leaderboard and purchases.

2. Information stored on your device

Your game progress (cleared heists, best scores, unlocked crew, trails, styles, loot, daily contracts and settings) is saved only on your device. It is removed if you clear the app's data or uninstall the game.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game is free and shows ads using Google AdMob. To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, AdMob may collect and process information from your device, including your device's advertising ID, IP address (which can indicate approximate location), device and app information, and how you interact with ads. Ads may be personalized based on this information where the law allows.

  • If you are in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, the game asks for your consent before personalized ads are shown, using Google's consent form. You can change your choice at any time with the "Ad privacy choices" button in the game's Settings.
  • Anywhere, you can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads in your device's settings (Settings > Google > Ads, or Settings > Privacy > Ads, depending on your device).
  • Learn how Google uses information from apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
  • Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

4. Google Play Games

The game uses Google Play Games Services for sign-in and the "Most Wanted" leaderboard. If you are signed in to Play Games, Google processes your Play Games profile (such as your player ID and display name) and the Endless scores you post, so they can be shown on the leaderboard to other players. You can manage your Play Games profile and visibility in the Google Play Games settings on your device. You can play the whole game without signing in to Play Games.

5. Purchases

The game offers optional in-app purchases (tips and crew packs). Payments are handled entirely by Google Play. We never see your card or payment details; Google tells the game only which items you own, so it can unlock them, including when you reinstall the game. See the Google Play Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

6. Permissions

The game uses an internet connection (for ads, the leaderboard and fonts) and your device's vibration motor for optional feedback, which you can turn off in Settings. It does not access your contacts, camera, microphone, precise location or files.

7. Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above.
